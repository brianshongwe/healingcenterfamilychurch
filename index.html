Who we are

A family learning to love God and love people.

Healing Centre Family Church is a non-denominational, non-cultural contemporary body of believers, united in loving God through serving People. Founded by Bishops Ephraim and Mirriam Mchunu in 1994, the church has grown to a membership of 2000+

Let us consider how we may spur one another on toward love and good deeds, not giving up meeting together. Hebrews 10:24–25
  • Rooted in Scripture. Every gathering is built around teaching that's honest, practical, and hopeful.
  • Genuinely welcoming. Come as you are — questions, doubts, and all. There's coffee and a seat with your name on it.
  • For the whole city. We serve Asheville through food pantries, mentoring, and neighborhood partnerships.

First time?

What to expect on Sunday

Walking into a new church can feel daunting. Here's exactly what your first visit will look like — no surprises.

01

A friendly hello

A greeter will meet you at the door, help you find coffee, and point the kids toward their check-in. Arrive whenever — there's no wrong time.

02

Worship & a message

Expect around 25 minutes of music followed by a down-to-earth, 30-minute message from the Bible. Come as you are — jeans are just fine.

03

Room to belong

Stay for coffee afterward and meet real people. No pressure to sign anything — just an open invitation to keep exploring together.
