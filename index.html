Our Story

A family united in loving God through serving people.

Healing Centre Family Church is a non-denominational, non-cultural contemporary body of believers, united in loving God through serving people.

Founded by Bishops Ephraim and Mirriam Mchunu in 1994, the church has grown to a membership of 2000+.
